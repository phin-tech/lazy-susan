- **Pickers show at once.** A dynamic group (Cubicle's workspace and window lists, say) now shows its items as soon as
  you enter it, even with the cheatsheet on a delay. Any group can do the same with `"showImmediately": true` in
  `config.json`, and a dynamic group can turn it off with `false`.
- When a dynamic group fails, it now shows the command's last error line rather than the first, so errors your shell
  prints at startup (fish's, say) no longer hide the real one.
