## Lazy Susan

A keyboard launcher for macOS. Press your leader shortcut, then a few keys, and an app opens, a URL loads, a folder
appears or a command runs. Shortcuts nest into groups, so `o m` can open Messages and `w m` maximize a window. This is
the first release.

Lazy Susan is a fork of [Leader Key](https://github.com/mikker/LeaderKey) by Mikkel Malmberg (MIT licensed). Nearly
all of it is his work. What's new here:

- **Which Key theme.** A Spacemacs-style panel along the bottom of the screen lists every key at the current level in
  columns. Groups are marked `+Name`, and a status line shows the keys you've pressed so far.
- **Radial theme.** A pie menu in the middle of the screen, with at most 8 keys per ring, two rings, then a list. A
  trail above the wheel shows the groups you've entered. Set Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Cheatsheet to *After …* and the
  wheel stays a small dot until you hesitate, so once you know your keys you never see it. `?` opens it any time.
- **Prompted actions.** Put `{input}` in a command or URL and Lazy Susan asks for text first: `cubicle rename {input}`
  renames a workspace, `https://duckduckgo.com/?q={input}` searches. What you type is never run as shell code.
- **Dynamic groups.** A group can fill itself from a command each time you open it, so it can list your current
  workspaces or windows.
- **Cubicle.** Cubicle's Settings can add a Cubicle group to Lazy Susan: go to or send a window to a workspace, pick
  a window, rename, snap. Other apps can do the same through `lazysusan://install`.
- **Runs beside Leader Key.** Lazy Susan has its own settings, config folder and `lazysusan://` URL scheme. To bring
  your shortcuts over, copy `~/Library/Application Support/Leader Key/config.json` into
  `~/Library/Application Support/Lazy Susan/`.
- **Updates.** Lazy Susan keeps itself up to date.

Lazy Susan needs macOS 13 Ventura or later, and the Accessibility permission for its global shortcut.
